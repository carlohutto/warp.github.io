Digital Equity Policy & Application

Stay Close Hotspot Program

Stay Close is WARP’s digital equity program. It connects Gaston County households that cannot afford internet access so families stay in touch, find employment, and reach vital services.

Program Guidelines & Dignity-First Policy

1. Who Can Join the Interest List

Gaston County residents who currently cannot afford reliable internet access. Priority is given to people recently released from incarceration and their families. Self-attestation is enough. No government ID, no proof of release, and no questions regarding background or past charges.

2. How It Works & What Participants Receive

Joining the interest list does not guarantee immediate hardware. When funding arrives, we contact applicants in priority order. Participants receive a mobile hotspot powered by a proposed partner agreement with Mission Telecom on the T-Mobile network.

3. Device Guidelines & Respect

Devices are provided for the active service period. Participants are encouraged (not required) to return the hardware if they no longer need it so another household can connect. Lost or damaged devices are replaced as funding allows, with no blame.

Official Program Disclaimer

Stay Close is a grassroots program run by volunteers at WARP, a small nonprofit with no outside funding. Hotspots and service are provided only as community donations allow, so we cannot guarantee immediate availability, timing, or length of service. Joining the interest list does not guarantee a device. Service is provided through proposed agreements with Mission Telecom on the T-Mobile network and is subject to provider terms and coverage limits.

Partnering for Inclusive Growth

WARP collaborates with local service providers, vendors, and businesses to construct meaningful reentry pathways. We prioritize engaging business partners who support fair-chance hiring practices and re-entry opportunities in Gaston County.
